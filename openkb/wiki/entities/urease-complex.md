# Urease Complex

## What it is

The urease complex is the coordinated set of urease subunits and accessory proteins encoded by *ureA* through *ureG*. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Evidence from ADP1 deletion phenotypes

In the ADP1 deletion collection, urea was the most demanding of the eight tested carbon sources: 97.9% of genes showed severe growth defects at a growth-ratio threshold below 0.5. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The top urea-specific genes were *ureA*, *ureB*, *ureC*, *ureD*, *ureE*, *ureF*, and *ureG*, representing all seven urease-complex subunits or accessory proteins examined in the analysis. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] This coordinated condition-specific signal identifies the urease system as a defining genetic requirement under urea growth conditions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The result provides a direct example of [[concepts/condition-dependent-essentiality]]: genes that may be dispensable in the overall deletion collection can become strongly important in a particular nutrient environment. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

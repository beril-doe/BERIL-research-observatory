# Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron

## Identity

**Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron** is a Bacteroidetes organism included among the 14 Fitness Browser organisms analyzed in the annotation-gap discovery study; its study shorthand was **Btheta**. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

## Findings in Annotation-Gap Discovery

*B. thetaiotaomicron* had 15 gapfilled enzymatic reaction–organism pairs, of which 3 were resolved, giving a resolution rate of 20.0%. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

This was the lowest organism-level resolution rate reported in the study, whose rates ranged from 20% in *B. thetaiotaomicron* to 71.4% in [[entities/klebsiella-michiganensis]]. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

The report characterized *B. thetaiotaomicron* as a Bacteroidetes organism with more divergent metabolism than the predominantly proteobacterial comparison set and interpreted its low resolution as consistent with phylogenetic distance from that majority. This interpretation is an extrapolation from the study's organism composition and resolution results, not a direct causal test. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

## Relevance

The result highlights [[concepts/organism-specificity]] as a limitation of [[concepts/evidence-triangulation]]: evidence integration resolved 47.8% of all 201 reaction–organism pairs, but performance was not uniform across organisms. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

It also motivates broader [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] across phylogenetically diverse organisms, because 12 of the 14 study organisms were Proteobacteria and the report identified limited phylogenetic breadth as a constraint on generalizability. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

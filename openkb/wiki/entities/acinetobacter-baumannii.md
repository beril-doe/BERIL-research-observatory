# Acinetobacter baumannii

## Identity

**Acinetobacter baumannii** is a bacterial species included in the within-species antimicrobial-resistance (AMR) variation study and is listed among six planned deep-dive case-study species. [src: amr_strain_variation]

## Relevance to the AMR Strain-Variation Report

The report identifies *A. baumannii* as a target for future detailed analyses combining UMAP visualizations, AMR heatmaps, and clinical metadata overlays. [src: amr_strain_variation]

No species-specific quantitative result for *A. baumannii* is reported in the current document. The proposed case study is intended to examine how its strain-level AMR profiles are organized, potentially in relation to the report’s broader themes of [[concepts/core-accessory-resistance]], [[concepts/resistance-islands]], and [[concepts/phylogenetic-amr-structure]]. [src: amr_strain_variation]

## Related Methods and Resources

The broader study identified AMR genes using [[entities/amrfinderplus]], measured strain relatedness with average nucleotide identity, and analyzed AMR profiles using distance-based clustering and phylogenetic Mantel tests. [src: amr_strain_variation]

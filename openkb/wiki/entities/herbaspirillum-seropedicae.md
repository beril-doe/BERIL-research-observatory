# Herbaspirillum seropedicae

## Identity

*Herbaspirillum seropedicae* is a bacterial organism included among the 14 Fitness Browser organisms analyzed for integrated metabolic annotation-gap discovery. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] Its study alias in the report is “HerbieS.” [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

## Role in Annotation-Gap Discovery

The study evaluated *H. seropedicae* using [[concepts/evidence-triangulation]] across ModelSEED gapfilling, Fitness Browser fitness evidence, pangenome conservation, GapMind, Bakta annotations, and BLAST homology. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] The organism was part of a multi-organism analysis designed to assess [[concepts/annotation-gap]] resolution through [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]] and [[concepts/pangenome-integration]]. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

Among the report’s organism-level results, *H. seropedicae* had 17 total annotation gaps, of which 10 were resolved, corresponding to a resolution rate of 58.8%. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] This rate placed it below *Klebsiella michiganensis* (71.4%), *Marinobacter* (66.7%), and *Azospirillum brasilense* (61.9%) in the listed organism comparison. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

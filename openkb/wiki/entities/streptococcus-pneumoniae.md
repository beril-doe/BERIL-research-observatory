# Streptococcus pneumoniae

## Identity

*Streptococcus pneumoniae* is a bacterial species included in the environmental resistome analysis. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

## Evidence from the environmental resistome analysis

- The dataset included **7,944 genomes** of *S. pneumoniae*. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- These genomes contained **59 AMR gene clusters**, of which **1 was core** and **58 (98%) were accessory**. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- The species was predominantly clinical in the dataset: **99.6%** of its genomes were classified as clinical. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- Its profile exemplifies the report's broader finding that clinical-dominated species tend to have highly accessory resistomes, a pattern relevant to [[concepts/core-accessory-resistance]] and [[concepts/environmental-resistome]]. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

## Interpretation

The *S. pneumoniae* case study is consistent with a clinical-environment association in which most detected AMR clusters occur in the accessory gene pool rather than the core genome. [src: amr_environmental_resistome] Because this is a species-level case study from unevenly sampled genomes, it supports—but by itself does not establish—a causal effect of clinical antibiotic exposure on AMR accumulation. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

## Related entities

The report presents *S. pneumoniae* alongside other deeply sampled clinical-associated species, including [[entities/klebsiella-pneumoniae]], [[entities/staphylococcus-aureus]], [[entities/salmonella-enterica]], and [[entities/mycobacterium-tuberculosis]]. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

# Marinobacter

## Overview

Marinobacter was one of the 14 organisms selected from the [[entities/fitness-browser]] for annotation-gap analysis using metabolic-model gapfilling and integrated genetic, pangenome, and sequence evidence. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

## Findings in the Annotation-Gap Study

The Marinobacter entry contained 12 gapfilled reaction–organism pairs. Eight pairs were assigned candidate genes, corresponding to a 66.7% resolution rate. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

This resolution rate placed Marinobacter among the better-resolved organisms in the study, although the report does not provide a Marinobacter-specific breakdown of high-, medium-, or low-confidence assignments. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

The result contributes to the study's broader finding that [[concepts/evidence-triangulation]] can resolve a substantial fraction of [[concepts/annotation-gap]] cases when gapfilling, fitness evidence, [[concepts/pangenome-integration]], and BLAST homology are combined. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

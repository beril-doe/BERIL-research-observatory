# Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Overview

*Mycobacterium tuberculosis* is a bacterial species included in the environmental resistome analysis. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

## Key facts

- The dataset contained **6,673 genomes** of *M. tuberculosis*. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- These genomes were associated with **44 AMR gene clusters**, including **4 core** clusters and **40 accessory** clusters. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- **91%** of its AMR clusters were accessory in the analyzed pangenome. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- The species was predominantly clinical, with **98%** of genomes classified as clinical. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]
- Its profile illustrates the report's broader association between clinical sampling and a highly [[concepts/core-accessory-resistance]] resistome. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

## Interpretation and limitations

The *M. tuberculosis* case study is consistent with the report's finding that clinically dominated species tend to carry substantial accessory AMR. [src: amr_environmental_resistome] This species-level observation is descriptive and does not by itself establish that clinical exposure caused the accessory AMR profile. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

The report's within-species analysis used a species-level proxy based on the fraction of genomes from clinical versus environmental sources rather than a true per-genome comparison. [src: amr_environmental_resistome]

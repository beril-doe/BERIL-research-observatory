# BERIL Knowledge Wiki

The **BERIL Research Observatory** is a corpus of AI-conducted microbial-biology research carried out over the KBase BER Data Lakehouse (BERDL) — a growing collection of project reports and cross-project digests that mine genomes, fitness screens, metagenomes, and multi-omics datasets for patterns in how microbes evolve, adapt, and function. The research spans pangenome-scale comparative genomics, RB-TnSeq fitness screening, metagenomic surveys of soils, guts, and subsurface habitats, and platform-level questions about how a data lakehouse under active migration can support reproducible, auditable science. Rather than a single narrative, it is a body of independently generated investigations that keep circling back to a shared set of hard problems: what genomes actually tell us about function, how confident we can be in inferences drawn from uneven or evolving data, and where the biggest gaps in current knowledge lie.

This wiki is organized so that **topics are the entry points**. Each of the 14 topics below synthesizes a cluster of related concepts drawn from across the project reports, giving you a guided way into the corpus without having to read every underlying report. From a topic page you can drill down into the **reference layers** — concepts (the recurring ideas and claims), entities (the organisms, tools, datasets, and platforms involved), and the underlying project reports themselves — to see exactly which findings support a given synthesis. The **Corpus** and **Browse** sections below give you other ways in: a full page catalog, and standing cross-cutting digests of discoveries and pitfalls surfaced across the whole observatory.

If you are new here, start with a topic that matches your interest, follow its concept links outward, and use the digests in Browse when you want the cross-project view rather than the topic-by-topic one.

## Topics

- [[topics/metabolic-capability-evolution|Metabolic Capability Evolution]] — How genomes gain, lose, hold in reserve, or diversify biosynthetic and catabolic pathways under selection, studied through pangenome-scale comparative genomics across *Pseudomonas* and other taxa.
- [[topics/conditional-gene-essentiality|Conditional Gene Essentiality]] — Gene essentiality as a graded, environment-dependent outcome that shifts with carbon source, stress, antibiotic exposure, and assay, not a fixed genomic label.
- [[topics/multi-omics-functional-integration|Multi-Omics Functional Integration]] — Combining DNA, RNA, protein, metabolite, phenotype, and model evidence to test whether a signal detected in one assay reflects a real functional state or an artifact of scale and condition.
- [[topics/bacterial-pangenome-dynamics|Bacterial Pangenome Dynamics]] — The pangenome as a dynamic system of conserved core, fluctuating accessory genes, and horizontally acquired or lost genes, studied through genome-scale and comparative-ecological evidence.
- [[topics/phylogenomic-trait-validation|Phylogenomic Trait Validation]] — Testing whether genome-derived traits like HGT, metal tolerance, niche, and phenotype correlations actually reflect independent biological reality rather than inference artifacts.
- [[topics/reproducible-data-provenance|Reproducible Data Provenance]] — Tracking dataset origin, currency, transformation, and re-executability on a lakehouse platform under active migration and multi-team development.
- [[topics/sampling-bias-in-metagenomics|Sampling Bias in Metagenomics]] — How uneven sampling across studies, locations, reference databases, and sequencing batches distorts environmental and ecological conclusions in metagenomics.
- [[topics/antimicrobial-resistance-genomics|Antimicrobial Resistance Genomics]] — How resistance genes are distributed across genomes, species, and ecosystems, and what that distribution implies about acquisition, retention, and fitness cost.
- [[topics/gene-function-prioritization|Gene Function Prioritization]] — Deciding which of the many functionally unannotated genes in large fitness-screening catalogs to test next, given finite experimental capacity.
- [[topics/subsurface-microbial-ecology|Subsurface Microbial Ecology]] — Comparative genomics and multi-angle study of energy-limited, hard-to-sample deep subsurface habitats like clay formations, boreholes, and contaminated groundwater.
- [[topics/cofitness-network-inference|Cofitness Network Inference]] — Using correlated fitness profiles from large-scale mutant screens, especially RB-TnSeq data in the Fitness Browser, to hypothesize functional relationships between genes.
- [[topics/microbial-metal-tolerance|Microbial Metal Tolerance]] — How bacteria survive and adapt to elevated copper, zinc, chromium, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury across contaminated soils, host tissue, and fitness assays.
- [[topics/protein-sequence-novelty|Protein Sequence Novelty]] — The degree to which protein sequences lack detectable evolutionary relatives or annotation, examined via a pangenome of over 130 million gene clusters, AlphaFold alignment depth, and InterProScan calls.
- [[topics/prophage-genome-ecology|Prophage Genome Ecology]] — Integrated bacteriophage genomes and their accessory cargo's role in reshaping host defense, competition, and niche range, studied through pangenome-wide gene-cluster detection.

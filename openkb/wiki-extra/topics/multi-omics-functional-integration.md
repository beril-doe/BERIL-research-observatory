# Multi-Omics Functional Integration

Multi-omics functional integration is the practice of combining DNA, RNA, protein, metabolite, phenotype, and computational-model evidence to ask whether a biological signal detected in one assay reflects a real, generalizable functional state or an artifact of that assay's particular scale, condition, or coverage. This corpus returns to the question again and again — in soils, guts, airway microbiomes, and single model organisms — because every one of its integrative projects finds that modalities agree only partially, and that the gaps between them are where the interesting biology (and the interpretive risk) lives. The topic sits at the center of the wiki: nearly every other concept eventually asks whether a claim survives contact with a second kind of measurement.

## What the Corpus Shows

**DNA and RNA can converge instead of diverging.** The intuitive expectation that RNA (active transcript pools) should be more sensitive to an environmental perturbation than DNA (persistent gene content) failed a direct multi-decadal test. At Harvard Forest, chronic +5°C soil warming produced comparable treatment R² for DNA and RNA (10–13%) once a horizon-by-incubation confound was removed, contradicting the RNA-leads-DNA hypothesis [src: harvard_forest_warming]. Directional but uncorrected signals — increased pmoA/pmoB transcripts and glyoxylate-cycle genes — still emerged, illustrating that omics convergence at the pool level does not erase gene-specific signal [src: harvard_forest_warming]. See [[concepts/dna-rna-functional-response]].

**Cross-modal agreement in model organisms is real but structurally partial.** The *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 resource integrates TnSeq essentiality, FBA flux, mutant fitness, and proteomics across 5,852 genes, but no single gene has data in all six modalities, and coverage ranges from 15% (FBA) to 58% (TnSeq) [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. Where FBA and TnSeq do overlap (866 genes), they agree 73.8% of the time [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer], and FBA-versus-knockout concordance is moderate (κ=0.486–0.493) [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]. Essentiality itself is not a fixed property: 499 genes are essential on minimal media versus 346 on LB [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer], and 70% of a triple-essentiality gene subset show condition-specific defects across eight carbon sources [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]. This is the empirical backbone of [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] and [[concepts/functional-redundancy]] — redundancy or essentiality can be true in one condition or resolution and false in another.

**Production and utilization are separable capabilities, not two views of the same fact.** In the FW300-N2E3 study, exometabolomic production data (Web of Microbes), gene fitness (Fitness Browser), pathway completeness (GapMind), and species phenotype records (BacDive) were cross-checked for 21 testable metabolites, yielding mean concordance 0.94 — but tryptophan was a clean discordance: increased in the exometabolome, backed by 231 significant fitness genes and a complete biosynthesis pathway, yet 0 of 50 *P. fluorescens* strains in BacDive utilized it as a carbon source [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]. This distinction — captured in [[concepts/metabolite-production-utilization-decoupling]] — shows why a single omics layer (e.g., "the gene is present" or "the metabolite is secreted") cannot stand in for a functional claim about consumption or catabolism.

**Community-level multi-omics can collapse onto one dominant axis, without collapsing the underlying mechanisms.** In the Crohn's disease phage-targeting project, a two-modality canonical correlation analysis over 106 subjects produced a first canonical pair (r=0.964) that simultaneously ordered six pathobiont species, ecotype-defining commensals, and dozens of metabolites along a single CD-associated direction, independently reproducing pathobiont-module structure found by co-occurrence networks [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Yet this single axis still contains at least two distinct mechanistic stories — iron/siderophore-driven *E. coli* specialization and bile-acid 7α-dehydroxylation by other Tier-A species — that require target-specific decisions downstream [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. See [[concepts/principal-direction-multi-omics]].

**Integration is driving practical design, not just description.** The CF airway formulation study used metabolic-overlap measurements to predict pathogen inhibition (r=0.384, R²=0.274), but cross-validation collapsed that predictive power (CV R²=0.145±0.142), and adding genus-level taxonomy raised training R² to 0.360 — meaning metabolism alone under-explains inhibition and non-metabolic mechanisms must be integrated [src: cf_formulation_design]. This motivates [[concepts/metabolic-competitive-exclusion]] as one input among several. Similarly, [[concepts/state-dependent-microbiome-therapy]] formalizes multi-omics integration as clinical logic: ecotype, target carriage, calprotectin, and bile-acid cost together determine cocktail choice, since a pooled clinical classifier only matched metagenomic ecotype calls 41% of the time in one cohort [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

**Community restructuring and functional-pool shifts are not the same evidence.** Labile-carbon addition to lignin-degrading enrichment cultures produced large, reproducible taxonomic shifts (Acinetobacter 25.2%→41.7%, Aeromonas 0.1%→20.2%) and reduced diversity (Shannon 3.16→2.41) [src: lignin_community_enrichment], but lignin loss, enzyme activity, and metabolite flux were never directly measured, so the functional consequence of the compositional shift remains a hypothesis pending [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] follow-up. See [[concepts/labile-carbon-priming]].

## Tensions and Caveats

The corpus does not converge on how much weight cross-omics agreement should carry. Several disputes are explicitly tracked as conflicts:

- FBA's relationship to real gene importance is contested: it achieves κ≈0.49 concordance with knockout lethality overall [src: adp1_triple_essentiality], yet fails to predict growth-defect severity among TnSeq-dispensable genes (p=0.63) and assigns 0% essentiality to Complex I genes it itself predicts carry 1.76× higher flux on aromatic substrates [src: aromatic_catabolism_network, adp1_triple_essentiality]. See [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--annotation_gap_discovery]].
- Estimates of how common condition-dependent essentiality is in ADP1 disagree by more than a factor of two — 31% by one deletion-matrix score versus 70% by a different threshold on a non-random gene subset — showing that method and gene-set choice alone can swing headline numbers. See [[conflicts/conflict--adp1_deletion_phenotypes--adp1_triple_essentiality--amr_cofitness_networks]].
- Whether Complex I essentiality tracks total NADH yield or NADH production rate is unresolved: quinate yields fewer theoretical NADH molecules than glucose yet makes Complex I essential, and NDH-2 presence does not reduce cross-species aromatic deficits as compensation theory predicts. See [[conflicts/conflict--aromatic_catabolism_network--respiratory_chain_wiring]].
- Functional redundancy itself is ambiguous: ENIGMA's genus-level contamination analysis found a robust null between metal contamination and a defense-function score (q=0.862 relaxed, q=0.849 strict) [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential], consistent with redundancy, while ADP1's respiratory-chain data show that apparently redundant dehydrogenases are in fact conditionally essential depending on carbon source [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] — leaving open whether redundancy is real, resolution-limited, or model-specification-dependent. See [[conflicts/conflict--enigma_contamination_functional_potential--respiratory_chain_wiring]].
- General cross-method discordance (FBA vs. TnSeq, GapMind vs. gapfilling, BacDive vs. exometabolomics) is argued to reflect both differing biological endpoints and genuine annotation incompleteness (only 47.8% of gapfilled pairs resolved) [src: annotation_gap_discovery, adp1_triple_essentiality]. See [[conflicts/conflict--adp1_triple_essentiality--amr_cofitness_networks--annotation_gap_discovery]].

A load-bearing limitation across nearly every project is coverage: no ADP1 gene has all six modalities measured [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer], only 21 of 58 FW300-N2E3 metabolites were cross-database testable [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency], and ENIGMA's genus bridge left 862 of 1,392 genera unmapped [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. Integration conclusions are therefore almost always conditional on a specific overlapping subset, not the full biological system.

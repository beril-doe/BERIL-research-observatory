================================================================================
ANALYSIS DESIGN
================================================================================

MODEL SPECIFICATION:
  Response: ko_present (0/1: genome lacks/carries KO)
  Exposure: mine_exposure = log₁₀(1 / (dist_km + 0.1))

COVARIATE LEVELS (Frisch-Waugh-Lovell residualization):
  L0 — bivariate (mine only)
  L1 — mine + pH + pH²
  L5 — L1 + WorldClim BIO1 + BIO12 + latitude
  L6 — L5 + C(genus)  ← MAIN TEST (genus fixed effects)

STATISTICAL METHOD:
  - Logistic regression per KO
  - Genus one-hot encoding (drop first for reference)
  - Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction
  - Threshold: q-value < 0.05

KO FILTERING:
  - Prevalence: ≥5 genera
  - Abundance: ≥10% of genomes (≥82 out of 818)
  - Sample size: ≥20 genomes per KO per level
  Expected: 5,000-10,000 KOs after filtering

================================================================================
RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS & PREDICTIONS
================================================================================

CENTRAL QUESTION:
  NB33 found 65-82% KO survival at genus PC1 control (CWM level).
  Does this replicate at MAG level with genus fixed effects?

PREDICTION A: SPIRE-LIKE COLLAPSE (Most Likely)
  - L6 survival: <5%
  - CWM signal driven by community composition, not gene-level selection
  - Precedent: SPIRE soil (3/99 KOs = 3% survival with genus FE)
  - Interpretation: Cannot decouple gene effects from taxonomic identity

PREDICTION B: CWM REPLICATION (Alternative)
  - L6 survival: 50-80%
  - Genuine gene-level adaptation independent of genus
  - Interpretation: Specific genes selected for mine proximity regardless of genus

GEOGRAPHIC STRATIFICATION:
  - EUR (521 MAGs): Concentrated Alpine mines → strong signal expected
  - USA (92 MAGs): Diffuse distribution → weak signal expected

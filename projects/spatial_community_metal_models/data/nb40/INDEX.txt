THESIS CONTEXT
================================================================================

This analysis addresses Part 3 of a 4-part thesis on metal ecology:

Part 1: Community composition responds to mine proximity ✓ (Completed)
Part 2: Functional gene diversity responds to mines ✓ (Completed)
Part 3: Do genes respond BEYOND community turnover? ← THIS ANALYSIS
Part 4: Multi-metal integration and synthesis (Pending)

Key Question: Is gene-level adaptation independent of which genera are present,
or is the signal purely driven by community composition (turnover)?

Expected Finding:
  - If <5% survival: Co-variation (SPIRE-like, turnover is primary)
  - If 50-80% survival: True adaptation (genes selected independent of genus)
  - Likely: Mixed (both turnover and selection contribute)

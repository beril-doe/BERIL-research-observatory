ANALYSIS DESIGN SUMMARY
================================================================================

RESEARCH QUESTION:
  Does NB33 CWM signal (65–82% KO survival with genus control)
  replicate at MAG level with genus fixed effects?

MODEL SPECIFICATION:
  Response: ko_present (0/1 per genome-KO pair)
  Exposure: mine_exposure = log₁₀(1 / (dist_km + 0.1))
  
  Levels:
    L0 — bivariate (mine only)
    L6 — mine + C(genus) [genus fixed effects, MAIN TEST]
  
  Method: Logistic regression with FDR correction (q < 0.05)
  Expected KOs after filtering: 5,000–10,000 (≥5 genera, ≥10% prevalence)

TWO POSSIBLE OUTCOMES:
  A) SPIRE-like collapse (<5% survival) → CWM signal is co-variation
  B) CWM replication (50–80% survival) → Genuine gene-level adaptation

THESIS CONTEXT:
  Part 3 of 4-part metal ecology thesis
  → Do genes respond beyond community turnover?

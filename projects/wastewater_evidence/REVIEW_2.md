## Evaluation Integrity

Nothing is fit in the machine-learning sense. Three thresholds (lift 50, purity 0.70, support 0.10) are chosen without tuning, and the report says so. Points that bear on how the numbers read:

- **The no-native hold-out is strict by construction.** A record is "no native signal" only if it carries no wastewater package, no wastewater ENVO annotation, no wastewater metagenome taxon, and no match to the generic keyword regex across 14 text fields. Any mined term that is itself generic wastewater wording (`sewage`, `activated sludge`, `wastewater treatment plant`) therefore cannot recover a no-native record. The only vocabulary that can is non-generic study idiom, and the project's own Scope says such idiom does not generalise across studies. The 1.2% and 0.5% figures are real, but part of what they measure is this design: the test asks whether one system's study idiom matches another system's studies. The report should state this when it says the result "refutes the simple version of the transfer idea" (What Would Have Changed Our Mind).
- **The GOLD-direction denominator is small and lopsided.** 381 records: 235 `industrial_ww` across 21 BioProjects (146 `Industrial wastewater > Unclassified`, 35 mine water, 30 hospital, 15 poultry, 9 AMD), 74 `wastewater_other`, 57 `bioreactor_anaerobic_sludge`, 11 digester, 3 activated sludge, 1 sludge. The 57 come from a GOLD group the Recommended scheme itself says is "AD-like but not stated as wastewater". The MGnify vocabulary tested against them is 94 terms mined from a cohort that is mostly activated sludge, nutrient removal and water-and-sludge. So the GOLD direction does carry a negative conclusion, as finding 7 says, but it is "94 MGnify terms do not match the idiom of about 30 GOLD studies, most of them industrial", not a general statement about thin-metadata wastewater records. The finding 7 heading, "Mined vocabulary transfers only where native signal already exists", is the general statement.
- **Accession-level overlap removal leaves study-level overlap.** The 589 doubly-labelled accessions are removed from the test sets, but the single-source term tables were built from all of each source's samples, and sibling accessions from the same BioProject can appear in the other source's test set. This can inflate the all-samples recovery figures (50.0% and 14.4%); it does not affect the no-native figures, which are the ones the headline uses. Worth a sentence.
- **Screen circularity** is now stated in finding 8, the figure title, the Limitations and discovery bullet 2, and quantified. Only the package leg is text-independent; the ENVO leg is circular for `env_*` terms (21 of 109 accepts, 2,588 records); the project leg is structure-dependent (2 accepts, 592 records). This is handled adequately.
- **Coherence reference** remains near-tautological for the package channel, which finding 9 and the Limitations say. See the `n_samples` semantics issue above.
- **Ascertainment** is stated in Scope: 9,390 accessions, 0.018% of BioSample, study-clustered, with the two MGnify clusters named. The GOLD side of the no-native set (235 industrial records over 21 BioProjects, largest 86) is not described there and should be, since finding 7 now rests on it.

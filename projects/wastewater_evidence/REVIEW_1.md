# Review: Wastewater Evidence Across Sample-Metadata Systems

## Summary

This is a careful, well-recorded exploratory project. Every cited run exited 0, every output hash in the cited `run.json` files still matches the file on disk, the four figures in `figures/` are byte-identical to `runs/20261017T015040Z-figures/out/`, and no number in the report traces to either superseded run. I recomputed the first crosstab table, the package table, the hold-out numbers, the coherence table, the trap table, the screen counts and the external-label table directly from the CSVs and parquet, and they agree with the report in every case I list below as verified. The report is honest about being exploratory and its Scope and Limitations sections are better than most. The main problems are interpretive rather than arithmetic: (1) the "independent support" screen in finding 8 is not independent of the term's own text for ENVO support on `env_*` field terms, which the report's own Limitations section concedes but finding 8 does not carry forward; (2) the cross-source hold-out does not exclude the 589 accessions labelled by both GOLD and MGnify, and more importantly its "no native signal" denominator is dominated by the very MGnify groups finding 6 calls mislabelled, so the 2.1% figure is partly the screen behaving correctly rather than transfer failing; (3) finding 10's "21%" is an arithmetic error from mixing denominators; (4) the attribution of GOLD's weak industrial-wastewater support to acid mine drainage is not what the outputs show; and (5) a handful of counts (field population sizes, the `env_package` statement, "17 fields", "aviary") are untraceable to any run output or misread one.

The four-channel design over all 51.7M BioSample records, the bridge of three external label sets to accessions, the enrichment-mined term table with cross-source hold-out, and the three label-free diagnostics are a sound and well-motivated sequence. Data sources are listed in the report's Data section with table names, and the two external fetches (GOLD Excel export, MGnify API) are recorded as runs with their raw downloads kept under `data/`.

**Reproducibility.** `README.md` has a `## Reproduction` section with the exact `beril run` commands in dependency order, notes that the documented Spark client cannot be installed and that `src/berdl_spark.py` opens a plain Spark Connect session, and says everything uses the cluster Python. There is no `requirements.txt`; the package list lives only in `memories/pitfalls.md`. Spark versus local separation is implicit: `01_gold_xlsx_to_parquet.py`, `01_mgnify_fetch.py` and `06_figures.py` run locally, everything else needs Spark. Runtimes are not stated; the logs show the biosample-evidence run at about 205 s and transfer-validate at about 138 s.

**Keyword channel is defined three different ways.** `02a_biosample_evidence.py` computes `kw` over 15 fields (its `TEXT_FIELDS` list has 17 entries but `kw_any` excludes `taxonomy_name` and `organism_name`). `ww_flags.TEXT_FIELDS`, used by `03_external_vs_biosample.py`, has 14 fields. `04_transfer_validate.py` and `04b_term_screen.py` concatenate 10 fields. **Small bugs and inconsistencies.**

- `02a_biosample_evidence.py` line 54: `min(w.how)` over `{'seed','descendant'}` always returns `'descendant'` alphabetically, so every row of `envo_terms.csv` says `descendant` and the `how` column is uninformative. Cosmetic.
- `ww_flags.ENVO_SEED_EXCLUDE_RE` explicitly excludes `oil sludge` and `marine sludge` from the seed set, but the subclass closure re-admits them (both are subclasses of `sludge`). Finding 3 then says the closure "correctly pulls in" oil sludge and marine sludge. The code's intent and the report's prose disagree. Oil sludge (petroleum residue) is not wastewater. Impact is 8 accessions in `envo_evidence_by_term.csv`, so immaterial to counts, but the prose should not call it correct.
- `04_transfer_validate.py` line 123: when a candidate value is missing from `content_pairs_aggregated`, `bg_count` is set to `n_total`, which yields the maximum possible lift (5,507.7, seen on `biomass from anaerobic digester`, `water trough`, `biomass from acid-phase anaerobic digester`). The log says 4 of 758 pairs were unresolved. This is a defensible choice but it silently promotes unresolved values to the top of the table.
- `04_transfer_validate.py` line 125: `n_total` counts label rows while `n_samples` counts distinct accessions; 747 accessions appear twice (GOLD and MGnify, or GOLD and NMDC), so lift is slightly inflated for values on doubly-labelled samples.
- Finding 5 says the 228,992 `digest` substring hits in `env_broad_scale` are "none ... in a wastewater package". `subtype_by_field.csv` shows `trap_digest_substring` / `env_broad_scale` with `n_with_pkg` = 5,831; it is the 222,412 `trap_digestive` subset that has 0. The sentence is true of the subset but reads as if it covers the whole.

## Evaluation Integrity

Nothing is fit in the machine-learning sense, but three thresholds are chosen (lift 50, purity 0.70, support 0.10) and a cross-source hold-out is reported, so items 1 to 4 and 6 of the checklist apply.

**Hold-out group leakage (item 3).** `labelled_samples.parquet` has 10,137 rows over 9,390 distinct accessions. 589 accessions carry both a GOLD and an MGnify label (and 158 both GOLD and NMDC). `04_transfer_validate.py` lines 153 to 163 test GOLD-built terms on all 4,352 MGnify rows without removing the 589 that are also in GOLD's training set. I recomputed with the overlap excluded, using the same `apply_terms` logic on the saved term tables:

| Direction | Reported | Overlap excluded | Reported no-native | No-native, overlap excluded |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GOLD to MGnify | 49.8% (n=4,352) | 50.0% (n=3,763) | 2.1% (n=729) | 1.2% (n=722) |
| MGnify to GOLD | 17.9% (n=5,583) | 14.4% (n=4,994) | 2.3% (n=388) | 0.5% (n=381) |

The leakage flatters the MGnify-to-GOLD direction by about 3.5 points and leaves the headline negative result intact or stronger. The report should state the overlap and report the clean numbers.

**Hold-out denominator is contaminated by the labels finding 6 rejects (item 1).** The 729 MGnify samples with no native signal break down as 372 `agricultural_ww`, 287 `industrial_ww`, 48 `nutrient_removal_bioreactor`, 19 `wastewater_other`, 3 `activated_sludge`. Finding 6 argues that the first two groups are mostly ducks, stream water and mine water. A wastewater vocabulary that recovers 2% of them is not failing; by the report's own account it should recover close to 0%. The sentence "Vocabulary transfer does not reach the hard cases" and the refutation bullet under What Would Have Changed Our Mind therefore rest on a denominator that is mostly not wastewater. The GOLD no-native set (388: 235 `industrial_ww`, 74 `wastewater_other`, 63 `bioreactor_anaerobic_sludge`, 11 `digester`, 4 `activated_sludge`, 1 `sludge_generic`) is the cleaner test, and 2.3% (0.5% clean) there does support a negative conclusion about reaching thin-metadata wastewater records. The negative result stands, but for the GOLD direction and for a narrower reason than stated.

**Screen circularity (item 7, annotation circularity).** `04b_term_screen.py` states support must "not come from the same text". `support_envo` is the rate at which records carrying the term have an ENVO wastewater term in `env_triads_flattened`. That table is parsed from the `env_broad_scale`, `env_local_scale` and `env_medium` strings. For a term mined from one of those fields, `support_envo` therefore asks whether the term's own text maps to a wastewater ENVO class. `term_screen.csv` shows it: `env_local_scale = sewage` has `support_pkg` 0.088, `support_proj` 0.055, `support_envo` 1.000, accepted. Of the 109 accepted terms, 81 clear the 0.10 bar on package support alone; 26 are accepted on ENVO support alone with package and project support both under 0.10, and 21 of those 26 are `env_*` field terms (4,347 records in total). The screen is still useful because package support does most of the work, but finding 8's "evidence that cannot come from its own text" and discovery bullet 2 overstate the independence. The Limitations section already says the ENVO triad is derived from the same text as the keyword channel; finding 8 needs the same sentence.

**BioProject co-membership leak (item 4).** `support_proj` is the fraction of a term's records whose BioProject contains at least one package-flagged sample. It is independent of the term's text but it is not independent of study structure: any term characteristic of a large project that used the wastewater package for some of its samples inherits support. In the outputs it is barely load-bearing: only 2 of 109 accepted terms rest on project support alone, `isolation_source = activated sludge sample` (479 records, 0.213) and `env_broad_scale = constructed feature` (113 records, 0.159). The second is the leak in miniature: a vague ENVO broad-scale label admitted because its projects also hold package samples. So the criterion leaks, but in this data the leak admits about 600 records.

**Coherence reference curve (item 4, baseline selection).** The package channel is a near-tautological reference: the package is chosen per submission, so within-project coherence is high by construction and measures assertion granularity, not correctness. The three curves also have different bases (`bioproject_coherence.csv` sums to 300,854 samples for package, 754,538 for keyword, 263,773 for term table, each restricted to projects where that channel fired at all). The 49 package projects under 20% hold 51,532 samples, which shows the admitted confound (large mixed studies) operating inside the reference itself. The diagnostic survives as a ranking and the report says so in Limitations, but "sets the reference" in finding 9 and discovery bullet 3 ("any derived flag can be scored against that reference curve") promote it to a scoring tool, which the report's own Limitations deny. A permutation null (shuffle flags within project, keep project sizes) would give a comparable baseline for all three channels.

**Ascertainment (item 6).** The labelled cohort is 0.018% of BioSample and is study-clustered: 338 of the 373 agricultural records are one BioProject (`Pekin Duck Water Sources`), all 192 riverine records are one study (`EcoImpacts stream water eDNA metagenome`), and the GOLD side is JGI-sequenced projects. The term table is therefore a vocabulary of a few dozen studies. The Scope section gives the fraction and says the result is not about wastewater in general, which is right; it does not say the vocabulary is study-specific, which is the practical consequence.

**Metric choice (item 2).** Recall-only hold-out with no precision estimate is acknowledged ("precision per subtype is unmeasured"). The trap set partially substitutes. Fine for an exploratory project.
